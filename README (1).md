# GST & Income Tax Reforms Dashboard (2025 vs 2026)

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard analyzing the impact of India's GST rate changes and income tax slab reforms year-over-year (2025 → 2026) — comparing old vs. new rates across goods categories and income slabs, and quantifying the resulting revenue impact, tax savings, and population affected.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard Screenshot](Screenshot_2026-04-21_122127.png)

## ✨ Key Insights Covered
- **Old vs New GST Rates** — comparison across goods categories (Essential, Standard, Luxury, etc.)
- **Revenue Impact by Category** — waterfall breakdown of increases/decreases leading to total impact
- **Old vs New Tax Slab vs Income** — how tax rates shift across income brackets (0–5 Lakhs up to 50+ Lakhs)
- **Population vs Tax Savings by Income Slab** — combo chart linking population size in each slab to savings generated
- **KPI Cards** — Net Revenue Impact, Total Tax Savings, and Population Benefited at a glance

## 🗂️ Files in this Repo
| File | Description |
|---|---|

| `GST_Tax_Reforms_2025.xlsx` | Source data for 2025 — GST rates and income tax slabs (pre-reform) |

| `Screenshot_2026-04-21_122127.png` | Static preview image of the dashboard |

## 📈 Data Fields
**GST Reforms sheet:** Category, Old/New GST Rate (%), Avg. Annual Sales (INR Crores), Projected Impact (%)

**Tax Reforms sheet:** Income Slab (INR Lakhs), Old/New Tax Rate (%), Population in Slab (millions), Avg. Tax Paid (INR Crores)
